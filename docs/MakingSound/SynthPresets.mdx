# Synth Presets

Each melodic track (Melody, Chords, Bass) has a library of synth sounds you can select. Use the **long-press gesture** on the mute pixel to browse and cycle through sounds.

:::info New in 1.1.2
The free sound library is bigger, **Pixelboop Pro** unlocks even more sounds, all sounds are level-matched with each other, and jams (including community jams) now remember each track's sound.
:::

### How to Change Sounds

1. **Long-press** the mute pixel (column 0) for the track you want to change.
2. After **0.5 seconds**, sound selection mode activates and the column switches to its bank-style display.
3. **Continue holding** — the display cycles to the next sound roughly every 0.7 seconds.
4. **Release** when you hear the sound you want.

The sound **previews automatically** as you cycle through the library. Free accounts cycle through the free sounds only — locked Pixelboop Pro sounds simply don't appear until [Pixelboop Pro](../GettingStarted/PixelboopPro) is unlocked.

## The Bank-Style Display

While cycling, column 0 no longer shows a single climbing dot — the free/Pro libraries are too big to fit one-pixel-per-sound in a 4-6 pixel column. Instead it shows two markers:

- A **dim pixel** marking which bank of sounds you're currently in.
- A **brighter pixel** marking your position within that bank.

When the two land on the same pixel, it lights at its **brightest**.

## Rhythm Track: Drum Kits

The **Rhythm track** does not have synth sounds. Instead it has **drum kits**, selected with the same long-press gesture on the rhythm track's mute pixel. The free **Synth** kit is the default; three sampled kits come with Pixelboop Pro.

| Kit | Tier | Character |
|-----|------|-----------|
| **Synth** | Free | Synthesized drums (default) |
| **Pulse** | Pixelboop Pro | Tight, electronic sampled kit |
| **Room** | Pixelboop Pro | Live room acoustic sampled kit |
| **Dusty** | Pixelboop Pro | Lo-fi, dusty sampled kit |

Within a kit, the drum sounds are organized into 4 banks. See [Drum Banks](DrumBanks) for information about the drum sounds.

## Level-Matched Volumes

All sounds are **level-matched** with each other, so switching sounds on a track doesn't jump the volume up or down — a quiet-sounding Flute and a loud-sounding Sync Lead play at the same perceived loudness.

## Jams Remember Your Sounds

Your sound selections are **automatically saved** and restored when you reopen the app. Each track remembers its last selected sound independently — and this now carries through to jams: opening a saved jam, or opening someone else's jam from Community Jams, brings back the sound each track was using.

## Unlocking More Sounds

The 25 free sounds above cover every track. To unlock the 23 additional sounds and the three extra drum kits (plus other Pixelboop Pro features), see **[Pixelboop Pro](../GettingStarted/PixelboopPro)**.
