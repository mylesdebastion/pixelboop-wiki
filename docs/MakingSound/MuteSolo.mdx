# Mute & Solo

The first two columns of the grid control track **muting** and **soloing**, letting you isolate or silence individual tracks during playback.

<MuteDemo />

| State | Visual | Audio | MIDI |
|-------|--------|-------|------|
| **Active** | Bright color | Playing | Sending |
| **Muted** | Dim color | Silent | Not sending |

Muting does not change a track's colour, it drops the whole column to about 16% brightness. The column keeps its gradient rather than becoming one flat shade. Measured on Pixelboop 1.1.3, top of each band to bottom:

| Track | Muted, top of band | Muted, bottom of band |
|---|---|---|
| Melody | `#1A0C25` | `#0B1128` |
| Chords | `#092328` | `#09260B` |
| Bass | `#252809` | `#282209` |
| Rhythm | `#281E12` | `#1F1212` |

### Mute Affects MIDI

When a track is muted:
- Internal audio is silenced
- **MIDI output is also stopped** for that track
- Notes remain in the pattern (just not played)

### Solo Behavior

- **Tap to solo**: Only that track plays
- **Tap again to unsolo**: All tracks return to normal
- **Solo multiple**: Tap multiple solo buttons to hear just those tracks

## Track Rows Reference

| Track | Rows | Mute Pixel | Solo Pixel |
|-------|------|------------|------------|
| Melody | 2-7 | (0, 2-7) | (1, 2-7) |
| Chords | 8-13 | (0, 8-13) | (1, 8-13) |
| Bass | 14-17 | (0, 14-17) | (1, 14-17) |
| Rhythm | 18-21 | (0, 18-21) | (1, 18-21) |

## Long-Press for Sounds

**Long-press** on the mute column (column 0) to enter [Synth Preset Selection](SynthPresets). This lets you change the sound of each track.

While cycling, the column switches to a **bank-style display**: a dim pixel marks which bank of sounds you're in, and a brighter pixel marks your position within it — brightest when the two coincide.
