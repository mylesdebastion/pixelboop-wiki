# Drum Banks

The Rhythm track uses **synthesized drums** organized into 4 banks. Each bank assigns different drum sounds to the 4 rhythm rows.

### The four columns to the left of the drum grid

Those narrow columns are controls, not labels. Nothing on the Rhythm lane names the
drums.

| Column | What it does on the Rhythm track |
|--------|----------------------------------|
| 0 | Mute. Tap to silence the drums. The level meter glows over this column as they play. |
| 1 | Solo. Tap to hear the drums on their own. |
| 2 | FX. Slide up or down to set the amount, tap to cycle Verb/Delay, Comp/Dist, Filter. |
| 3 | Drum bank. Tap anywhere in this column to move to the next bank. The bright cell is the bank you are on, the three dim ones are the banks you are not. |

Column 2 shimmers while the effects are working, so its color is never the same twice.
Judge it by how far up the column is filled, not by its shade.

To hear which drum a row plays, tap a step in that row: the hit goes in and the drum's
name (Kick, Snare, Closed Hat) flashes up. Switching banks announces the new bank the
same way, as a four-word shorthand like "KD SD CH TM".

## General MIDI Drum Map

When using MIDI with external gear, the rhythm track sends standard GM drum notes on **Channel 10** (default).

| Note | Drum Sound |
|------|------------|
| 35 | Acoustic Bass Drum |
| 36 | Bass Drum 1 |
| 37 | Side Stick |
| 38 | Acoustic Snare |
| 39 | Hand Clap |
| 40 | Electric Snare |
| 42 | Closed Hi-Hat |
| 46 | Open Hi-Hat |
| 47 | Low-Mid Tom |
| 49 | Crash Cymbal 1 |
| 51 | Ride Cymbal 1 |
| 56 | Cowbell |

These mappings follow the General MIDI (GM) standard, ensuring compatibility with GM-compliant synths and DAWs.
