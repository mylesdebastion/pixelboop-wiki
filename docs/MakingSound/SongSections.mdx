# Song Sections

Pixelboop supports **8 song sections** (pattern banks) for building complete songs. Each section stores an independent pattern for all 4 tracks.

<SectionPlayDemo />

### Section Play Behavior

When **Section Play Mode** is enabled:
1. Pattern plays through all 32 steps
2. At step 32, advances to the next section
3. Cycles through all 8 sections
4. Loops back to section 1 after section 8

This creates a **256-step song** (8 sections × 32 steps).

## Building a Song

### Workflow

1. **Start in Section 1**: Create your intro or verse pattern
2. **Copy to Section 2**: Duplicate and modify for variation
3. **Section 3**: Build a chorus or bridge
4. **Continue**: Fill sections 4-8 with song parts
5. **Enable Section Play**: Listen to the full song

### Arrangement Ideas

| Section | Purpose | Example |
|---------|---------|---------|
| 1 | Intro | Sparse melody, no drums |
| 2 | Verse 1 | Add bass and light drums |
| 3 | Chorus | Full energy, all tracks |
| 4 | Verse 2 | Variation of section 2 |
| 5 | Chorus | Same as section 3 |
| 6 | Bridge | Different feel, buildup |
| 7 | Chorus | Final chorus, extra energy |
| 8 | Outro | Strip back, fade out |

**How to merge:**
1. **Copy** a section (hold 1s)
2. **Tap** a different section column
3. See **"MERGED"** tooltip
4. Target section switches to active
5. Target section now contains notes from **both** sections (additive merge)

### Additive Merge Behavior

When merging sections:
- **Existing notes are preserved**: Notes in the target section stay
- **New notes are added**: Notes from the copied section are added
- **Overlapping notes**: If both sections have a note at the same position, the copied note replaces it
- **All tracks merged**: Melody, Chords, Bass, and Rhythm all merge

### Use Cases

#### Creating Variations
1. Copy section 1 (intro)
2. Merge to section 2
3. Add extra notes to section 2 (variation)

#### Building Up Arrangements
1. Section 1: Just melody
2. Copy section 1, merge to section 2
3. Add bass to section 2 (melody + bass)
4. Copy section 2, merge to section 3
5. Add drums to section 3 (melody + bass + drums)

#### Combining Song Parts
1. Section 1: Verse melody
2. Section 2: Chorus chords
3. Copy section 1, merge to section 3
4. Copy section 2, merge to section 3
5. Section 3 now has both verse melody AND chorus chords

### Undo Support

Merging is **fully undoable**:
- Press **Undo** (row 0, column 4) to restore the pre-merge state. Redo is the next pixel along, column 5.
- Or use **double-swipe left** gesture for quick undo

### Section Variation (Tap-Tap)

If you tap a section and then **tap an empty section**, you get a **variation** (not a merge):
1. **Tap** section 1 (active)
2. **Tap** section 2 (empty)
3. See **"SEC VARIATION"** tooltip
4. Section 2 gets a copy of section 1 (traditional copy, not merge)

**Difference from Copy-Merge:**
- **Variation**: Replaces target section (overwrite)
- **Copy-Merge**: Adds to target section (additive)
