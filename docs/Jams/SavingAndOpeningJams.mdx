# Saving & Opening Jams

A **jam** is a recording of a Pixelboop session: your pattern, tempo, key, and the notes you played, captured as a loop you can return to later.

## Saving is something you do

Pixelboop records everything you play, but a jam is only kept once you save it. Stopping playback saves nothing. Two ways to save: **hold** the purple Jams button in the top-right corner (this saves the current jam and clears the grid for a new one), or open the jam library and tap **Save** on the top row. A session with fewer than five recorded events is discarded rather than saved.

One thing does happen on its own: if a recording checkpoint is more than 24 hours old, Pixelboop finalises and saves it on next launch.

## Opening and managing your jam library

The jam library (browsing, reopening, and managing your saved jams) is included in Pixelboop. Publishing a jam to the public community gallery is a separate feature and is still rolling out.

1. Open the **jam menu** from the control bar to see your jam library.
2. Your saved jams appear as a list with names, dates, and grid thumbnails.
3. **Tap a jam** to load it back onto the grid, exactly as you left it, then keep playing or build on it.

## Starting a new jam

Starting a **new jam** clears the grid for a fresh session. Your previous jam stays saved, so you can always reopen it from the library.

:::tip
To get a jam off your device (share it with others, or open it in a DAW), see **[Community Jams](./CommunityJams)** and **[Converting Jams to MIDI](./ConvertingJamsToMidi)**.
:::
