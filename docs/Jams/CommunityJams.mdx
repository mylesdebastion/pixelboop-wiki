# Community Jams

Community Jams are jams the Pixelboop community has shared publicly. Browse the gallery, open any jam in the app, or download it, all without needing an account.

## Browsing the gallery

Browse everything the community has shared at **[pixelboop.com/jams](https://pixelboop.com/jams)**. Each jam has its own page at `pixelboop.com/j/<id>` where you can:

- **Open in Pixelboop** — opens the jam directly in the app so you can play and remix it.
- **Download the `.pbjam`** file (the Pixelboop jam format).
- **Download MIDI** to open it in a DAW. See **[Converting Jams to MIDI](./ConvertingJamsToMidi)**.

:::tip
Jams open with their saved sounds, so a community jam plays back the way its creator heard it, not just its pattern. Jams saved before this feature arrived fall back to the default sounds.
:::

## Publishing your own jams
