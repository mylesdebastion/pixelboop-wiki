# Pixelboop Pro (Coming Soon)

Everything in the current version of Pixelboop is **included free**: all 48 sounds across Melody, Chords, and Bass, the classic Synth drum kit, FX, harmonic colors, and every gesture on the grid.

**Pixelboop Pro** is an optional add-on arriving in a future update. It will be a **one-time purchase**, not a subscription: buy it once and it's yours.

## What Pro will add

- **Extra drum kits**: sampled kits for the Rhythm track, alongside the classic Synth kit.
- **[LiteJam Guitar](../Connectivity/LiteJamGuitar) support**: pair a LiteJam LED guitar and follow along on its fretboard.
- **Per-instrument hardware visualizations**: each instrument driving its own external LED lights. See [WLED Setup](../Connectivity/WLED) for what's possible with LED strips today.

Nothing in the current version moves behind Pro. Until Pro ships, there is nothing to buy: just make music.
