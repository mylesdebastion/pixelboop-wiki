# Unlocking Pixelboop Pro

Pixelboop includes a full free sound library — 25 sounds across Melody, Chords, and Bass — right out of the box. **Pixelboop Pro** is an optional one-time in-app purchase that unlocks even more.

:::info New in 1.1.2
Pixelboop Pro is now available to unlock from inside the app.
:::

## What's included

- **The rest of the sound library** — 7 more sounds across Melody, Chords, and Bass. See [Synth Presets](../MakingSound/SynthPresets) for the full free/Pro breakdown per track.
- **[LiteJam Guitar](../Connectivity/LiteJamGuitar) support** — pair a LiteJam LED guitar and follow along on its fretboard.
- **Per-instrument WLED visualizations** — see [WLED Setup](../Connectivity/WLED) for what's possible with external LED strips.

Pixelboop Pro is a **one-time purchase**, not a subscription — buy it once and it's yours.

## Unlocking Pro

1. Open the **Rainbow menu** (the rainbow button on the control bar).
2. Find the **Sound Library** section. Locked Pixelboop Pro sounds are listed by name alongside the free ones.
3. Tap **Unlock**. This opens the purchase sheet.
4. Complete the purchase. Pro sounds and features are available immediately.

## Restoring a previous purchase

Already bought Pixelboop Pro on another device, or reinstalled the app? Open the purchase sheet from the **Sound Library** section and tap **Restore Purchases** — no need to pay again.

:::tip
Pixelboop Pro supports **Family Sharing**. If it's been purchased by someone in your Family Sharing group, you can unlock it on your device via Restore Purchases at no extra cost.
:::
