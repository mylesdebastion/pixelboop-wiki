# LiteJam LED Guitar

Pixelboop pairs with the **LiteJam LED guitar** over Bluetooth and lights up its fretboard to show you what to play, turning your jam into a guided, hands-on guitar experience.

:::info Coming with Pixelboop Pro
LiteJam Guitar support is not yet available in the current version of Pixelboop. It arrives with **[Pixelboop Pro](../GettingStarted/PixelboopPro.mdx)** in a future update.
:::

## Connecting your LiteJam guitar

1. Power on your LiteJam guitar and keep it nearby.
2. In Pixelboop, open the **Rainbow Menu → Visualizer** group and find the **LiteJam** section.
3. Tap **scan**. Pixelboop looks for nearby LiteJam guitars over Bluetooth.
4. Select your guitar to pair. The status moves through **Connecting...** to **Connected**.

Make sure Bluetooth is on, and allow Pixelboop to use it if prompted.

## Playing with the LED fretboard

Once connected, Pixelboop drives the guitar's LEDs in time with your jam, highlighting the notes and chords on the fretboard so you can follow along and play.

:::tip
The **Harmonic Colors** option (in the Rainbow Menu → Interface group) also applies to the LiteJam fretboard: notes are colored by the circle of fifths, so consonant notes (4ths and 5ths) share similar colors and the tritone stands out.
:::
