# Tracks & Sounds

PixelBoop is organized into four distinct musical tracks. Each track occupies a horizontal section of the 44x24 grid.

## The Four Tracks

1.  **Chords**: The foundation of your sound.
2.  **Melody**: The lead voice.
3.  **Bass**: The low-end energy.
4.  **Drums**: The rhythmic pulse.

## Toggling Steps

Simply tap a pixel to toggle it on or off. The color of the pixel reflects the track it belongs to.
- **Pink**: Chords
- **Blue**: Melody
- **Green**: Bass
- **Yellow**: Drums
