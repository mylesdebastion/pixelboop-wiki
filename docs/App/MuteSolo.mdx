# Mute & Solo

The first two columns of the grid control track **muting** and **soloing**, letting you isolate or silence individual tracks during playback.

| State | Visual | Audio | MIDI |
|-------|--------|-------|------|
| **Active** | Bright color | Playing | Sending |
| **Muted** | Dim color | Silent | Not sending |

### Mute Affects MIDI

When a track is muted:
- Internal audio is silenced
- **MIDI output is also stopped** for that track
- Notes remain in the pattern (just not played)

### Solo Behavior

- **Tap to solo**: Only that track plays
- **Tap again to unsolo**: All tracks return to normal
- **Solo multiple**: Tap multiple solo buttons to hear just those tracks

## Track Rows Reference

| Track | Rows | Mute Pixel | Solo Pixel |
|-------|------|------------|------------|
| Melody | 2-7 | (0, 2-7) | (1, 2-7) |
| Chords | 8-13 | (0, 8-13) | (1, 8-13) |
| Bass | 14-17 | (0, 14-17) | (1, 14-17) |
| Rhythm | 18-21 | (0, 18-21) | (1, 18-21) |

## Long-Press for Presets

**Long-press** on the mute column (column 0) to enter [Synth Preset Selection](/docs/App/SynthPresets). This lets you change the sound of each track.
