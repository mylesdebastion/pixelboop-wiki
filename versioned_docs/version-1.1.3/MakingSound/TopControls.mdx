### Double-Tap: Restart to Step 1

Double-tapping the Play button restarts the pattern from step 1.

- **While playing**: playback keeps going, but jumps back to step 1.
- **While paused**: the playhead resets to step 1 and a **"stop / reset"** tooltip confirms it.
- **In a WiFi jam session**: the restart syncs to every connected player, so the whole session snaps back to step 1 together.

A single tap still toggles play/pause as normal — Pixelboop waits briefly to see if a second tap follows before acting.

### Hold for the Rainbow Menu (Shortcut)

**Hold the Play button for about three and a half seconds** and the **Rainbow menu** opens. A firm haptic tap tells you it caught. This is a shortcut for when your hand is already at the top-left of the grid; the usual way in is to **tap the rainbow gradient in the bottom-left corner** (row 23, cols 0-3). Both gestures land on exactly the same screen.

It is a full screen, not a small dialog with a couple of fields on it. Grid labels, note colors, every light Pixelboop can drive, and the app's own about and licenses all live in there, and the lighting settings (including the address of a WLED strip) sit a level in rather than on the first thing you see. Rather than repeat that layout here, see [The Rainbow Menu](../GettingStarted/RainbowMenu.mdx).

For wiring up LED strips end to end, see the [WLED Setup Guide](../Connectivity/WLED.mdx).

### The Jams Button (Cols 40-43)

The four purple pixels in the top-right corner are your jam library, not a reset button.

- **Tap**: opens the jam library. Your saved jams are listed with names, dates, and thumbnails, and tapping one loads it back onto the grid.
- **Hold**: saves whatever you have been playing, then clears the grid so you can start something new. A firm haptic tap and a **"NEW JAM"** tooltip confirm it. The hold is short (well under a second), so press and stay put rather than tapping twice.

The purple starts dim and brightens once you have at least one jam saved, so the button tells you at a glance whether the library has anything in it. The figure above shows the dim, empty-library state.

Two things worth knowing before you hold it:

- If you have barely played anything (fewer than five recorded events), there is nothing worth keeping. The grid still clears, but no jam is added to the library.
- If a save genuinely fails, Pixelboop leaves your grid alone. You get a **"Save failed! Jam kept for recovery"** tooltip and your playing stays on screen instead of being wiped.

:::note Coming in the next version
Tapping the Jams button while the library is already open will close it again. Until that ships, each tap opens another copy of the library on top of the last one, and every copy needs its own **Done** tap to clear.
:::

For the whole story on saving, reopening, and starting fresh, see [Saving & Opening Jams](../Jams/SavingAndOpeningJams.mdx).
