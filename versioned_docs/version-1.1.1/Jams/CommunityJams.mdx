# Community Jams

Community Jams let you publish a finished jam to the public **[Pixelboop gallery](https://pixelboop.com/jams)** and share it with a link, so anyone can hear it, open it, or remix it.

:::info Available in TestFlight
Publishing to Community Jams is currently rolling out to testers. Want to share your jams early? Join the **[Early Creator program](https://pixelboop.com/community)** to help shape Pixelboop.
:::

## Turning on Community Jam publishing

1. Open the **Rainbow menu** (the rainbow button on the control bar).
2. Find the **Community Jam** section and turn on **auto-publish**.
3. From then on, when you start a new session, your finished jam is automatically saved to the public Community Jam gallery.

## Sharing a jam

Each published jam gets its own page at `pixelboop.com/j/<id>`. From that page, anyone can:

- **Open in Pixelboop** to play and remix it.
- **Download the `.pbjam`** file (the Pixelboop jam format).
- **Download MIDI** to open it in a DAW. See **[Converting Jams to MIDI](./ConvertingJamsToMidi)**.

Browse everything the community has shared at **[pixelboop.com/jams](https://pixelboop.com/jams)**.
