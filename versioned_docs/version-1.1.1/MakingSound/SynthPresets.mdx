# Synth Presets

Each melodic track (Melody, Chords, Bass) has multiple synth presets you can select. Use the **long-press gesture** on the mute pixel to browse and select sounds.

### How to Change Presets

1. **Long-press** the mute pixel (column 0) for the track you want to change
2. After **0.5 seconds**, preset selection mode activates
3. A **bright indicator** appears at the current preset
4. **Continue holding** — the indicator moves to the next preset every 0.7 seconds
5. **Release** when you hear the sound you want

The sound **previews automatically** as you cycle through presets.

## Preset Persistence

Your preset selections are **automatically saved** and restored when you reopen the app. Each track remembers its last selected preset independently.
